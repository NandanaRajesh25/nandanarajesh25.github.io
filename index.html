🚀 What Is dashcall?

dashcall is an application that connects to your Claude Code sessions. Claude Code is a tool that helps developers work with AI. Normally, you would sit at your computer to use Claude Code. With dashcall, you can step away from your desk and still talk to your sessions using your phone or even your car's web browser.

Imagine you are cooking dinner and want to check on a coding task. Or you are driving and need to review something hands-free. dashcall makes that possible. It uses voice recognition to turn your speech into text, and it can read responses back to you aloud. You can also use touch controls on your phone screen if you prefer.

The application is designed to be friendly for non-technical users. You do not need to understand how the AI works under the hood. You just need to download it, open it, and follow the instructions.

✨ Key Features

  • Voice and touch control: Speak naturally or tap buttons on your phone screen. Use whichever is easier for you.
  • English and Turkish: dashcall understands and speaks both languages. You can switch between them as needed.
  • Phone and car browser access: Open a web browser on your phone or your car's dashboard screen to reach dashcall. No special app store download is required for the phone side.
  • Speech-to-text: Your spoken words become text commands for Claude Code. This is powered by Whisper, a reliable speech recognition technology.
  • Text-to-speech: Claude Code's responses are read aloud to you, so you can listen instead of reading a screen.
  • Secure connection: dashcall uses Tailscale to create a private network between your computer and your phone. This keeps your sessions safe from outsiders.
  • Works as a progressive web app: You can add dashcall to your phone's home screen for quick access, just like a regular app.

📱 How dashcall Works

Here is the basic idea. You run dashcall on your Windows computer. This computer is the "host" that talks to Claude Code. Then, you open a web browser on your phone or car and connect to the dashcall host. Because both devices are linked through a secure private network, you can send voice commands and hear responses from anywhere with an internet connection.

You do not need to install anything on your phone. Just use the browser you already have. This makes dashcall very convenient and easy to try.

The application handles all the technical details behind the scenes. You just speak or tap, and dashcall takes care of turning your voice into text, sending it to Claude Code, and reading the answer back to you.

🖥️ System Requirements

Before you download, make sure you have the following:

  • A Windows computer (Windows 10 or Windows 11 recommended).
  • A modern web browser such as Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Mozilla Firefox.
  • A stable internet connection.
  • A Claude Code account that is already set up and working.
  • A phone or car browser with internet access for remote control.

dashcall is lightweight and does not require a powerful computer. If your computer can run a web browser, it can probably run dashcall.

    🎙️ Using dashcall

    Once your setup is finished, using dashcall is straightforward. Here is how you get the most out of it.

    Start a session

    Open the dashcall address on your phone or car browser. You will see a simple control screen. To begin talking to your Claude Code session, tap the microphone button or press the talk control on your screen.

    Speak your request

    Speak clearly and naturally, just as you would to a person. dashcall converts your speech to text using Whisper and sends it to Claude Code. Wait a moment for the response.

    Listen to the reply

    Claude Code's response is read aloud to you automatically. You can adjust the volume using your device's volume buttons. If you prefer to read the text, it also appears on the screen.

    Switch between English and Turkish

    In the dashcall settings, you can choose your preferred language. You can switch at any time, either before or during a session. Both languages are fully supported for voice input and spoken replies.

    Use touch controls

    If speaking is not convenient, you can type your request on the on-screen keyboard or use the buttons provided. Touch control is great for quiet environments or when you just want to tap instead of talk.

    Add to home screen for quick access

    On your phone, open the dashcall web address in your browser and use the browser menu to "Add to Home Screen." This creates an icon that opens dashcall like a regular app.

    🔒 Privacy and Security

    Your conversations with Claude Code are private. dashcall relies on Tailscale to build a secure private network between your computer and your phone. This means your traffic travels through an encrypted tunnel that only your own devices can access. Random people on the internet cannot see your session.

    Voice data is processed only to convert your speech into text. Your session content is not shared with third parties beyond what is needed to reach Claude Code itself. Follow good password habits and keep your Tailscale account secure to get the best protection.

    🧰 Troubleshooting

    The download page did not open.

    Check your internet connection and try again. You can also copy the link and paste it directly into your browser address bar.

    The application will not start.

    Make sure the download finished completely. If Windows shows a security prompt, click "More info" and then "Run anyway" if you trust the source. Restart your computer and try again.

    My phone cannot reach dashcall.

    Confirm that both your computer and your phone are connected to the same Tailscale network. Also verify that you typed the web address exactly as shown during setup.

    Voice input is not working.

    Allow microphone permissions in your phone or car browser. Make sure your microphone is not broken or muted by another app.

    Responses are not read aloud.

    Check your device volume. Also verify that text-to-speech is enabled in the dashcall settings.
